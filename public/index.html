About regrò

Agroecology and Web3

Since its inception, the regrò project is consistently collaborating with blockchain-based protocols and solutions built to resolve the value chain fallacies in Agriculture, VCMs and Biodiversity protection.

Food forests and self-sovereignty

From established permaculture gardens to small orchards under the forest's canopy, regrò supports any effort to protect soils and forests, grow food, implement solar energy facilities and catch/reuse rain water.

Art and the Land

regroers are artists and art collectors as well! Through our virtual and physical outlets we are committed to share the connection with Nature via compelling works of art.
